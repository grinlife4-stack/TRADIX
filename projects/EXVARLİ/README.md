# EXVARLİ

**Parent:** TRADIX  
**Project type:** Independent financial management and operations platform  
**Status:** Project dossier created; implementation not yet started  
**Display name:** EXVARLİ  
**Purpose:** Provide a controlled, auditable, and replaceable financial management layer for multiple independent projects.

## 1. Mission

EXVARLİ is an independent financial management platform designed to record, reconcile, oversee, and report financial activity for multiple projects, including projects that may be added in the future.

EXVARLİ provides financial management services; it does not become the owner of the projects it serves, their income, their funds, or their financial assets. Each participating project retains its own identity, ownership, financial records, and operational independence.

EXVARLİ is not intended to be a public bank, public exchange, payment processor, or custodian for unrelated third parties.

## 2. General ownership, independence, and continuity principles

These principles apply to every current or future project supported by EXVARLİ. They are not limited to any named project or initial portfolio.

- **Ownership remains with the project:** All income, balances, bank accounts, payment accounts, wallets, and other financial assets must remain legally and operationally owned or controlled by the individual or legal entity designated as the owner of the relevant project.
- **No asset ownership by EXVARLİ:** EXVARLİ acts only as an authorized financial manager/service provider. It must not become the owner or beneficial owner of project income, bank accounts, wallets, or other project assets merely because it records, reconciles, administers, or reports them.
- **Separate financial records:** Every project must retain its own ledger, transaction history, supporting documents, account references, and reconciliations. Consolidated reporting is permitted only if the underlying project records and ownership remain clearly separated.
- **Data portability:** Each project’s data, records, receipts, audit history, and reconciliation information must be exportable in documented, commonly usable formats and transferable to the project owner or a replacement financial manager.
- **No lock-in:** EXVARLİ must not create technical, contractual, or operational dependencies that prevent a project owner from accessing or transferring its data, income, accounts, wallets, or assets.
- **Replaceable management:** The project owner must be able to terminate or change EXVARLİ’s authorization and appoint another financial manager or operate the financial process independently, subject to applicable law and provider requirements.
- **Continuity after separation:** The sale, suspension, shutdown, restructuring, or replacement of EXVARLİ must not interrupt the operation of supported projects or block their access to income, accounts, wallets, assets, or records.
- **Limited and revocable access:** EXVARLİ receives only the permissions necessary to perform its agreed duties. Permissions must be revocable by the relevant project owner, and access must be logged and auditable.
- **No commingling:** Funds and financial records belonging to different projects must not be pooled, mixed, or treated as EXVARLİ’s own assets. Any authorized inter-project transfer must be separately documented and must not be misclassified as new income.

The legal ownership structure and account holder for each project must be explicitly identified before a financial account, wallet, payment route, or provider integration is activated. EXVARLİ’s administrative role does not itself establish ownership or authority over those assets.

## 3. Initial platform scope

The initial platform scope is limited to:

- Recording income by source and by project.
- Recording gross amounts, deductions, net amounts, receipts, settlement status, and timestamps.
- Maintaining a traceable audit history for every financial operation.
- Supporting approved provider integrations for receiving funds and, only where lawful and contractually permitted, recording or initiating transfers to accounts or wallets owned or controlled by the relevant project owner.
- Recording conversion provider, exchange rate, fees, asset received, destination address, network, and transaction identifier when applicable.
- Producing project-level records and consolidated portfolio reports without mixing project balances or ownership.
- Providing complete export and handover capability for each project independently.

## 4. Initial income sources under consideration

Initial examples under consideration include:

- Google advertising revenue generated by a portfolio project.
- Affiliate revenue generated by a portfolio project through programs such as Amazon or Alibaba.
- Future income sources generated by any additional project supported by EXVARLİ, subject to explicit approval and compliance review.

These are examples of potential integrations, not claims that any provider account or payment route is already connected or approved. The platform’s architecture must remain open to additional projects and income sources without making them dependent on any one named project.

## 5. Accounting and data boundaries

- Each project must have its own financial ledger and source records.
- Portfolio-level reporting may aggregate data but must preserve project attribution and must not merge ownership or underlying ledgers.
- Transfers between projects are internal transfers, not new income.
- No project-specific financial account, wallet, or ledger is to be instantiated or connected until explicitly authorized by the relevant project owner.
- Every balance must be derivable from recorded, reconciled events; balances must not be silently edited.
- Records must retain sufficient provenance to support audit, reconciliation, export, and handover to another manager.

## 6. Explicit exclusions for the initial release

- Public customer accounts or wallets.
- Public deposits, withdrawals, or payment services.
- Custody or beneficial ownership of project assets by EXVARLİ.
- Lending, credit, or interest-bearing products.
- A public cryptocurrency exchange.
- Unapproved or unsupported conversion routes.
- Unnecessary features unrelated to the initial financial management mission.

## 7. Compliance boundary

A transparent source of income does not by itself establish that a payment, conversion, custody, or transfer route is lawful or permitted by a provider. Each route must be checked against applicable jurisdictional requirements, provider terms, sanctions restrictions, account-holder eligibility, and asset/network support before activation.

The account holder, beneficial owner, source of funds, and authorized operator must be accurately represented to each financial institution and provider. EXVARLİ’s management role must not be used to obscure the actual owner of funds or assets.

Iranian tax analysis is outside the current project scope, as expressly specified by the project owner.

## 8. Architecture direction

The intended architecture is a shared financial management core with strictly separated project ledgers, project-scoped permissions, portable records, and replaceable integration adapters for income sources, payment providers, and conversion providers.

The system must preserve immutable operation history, verifiable reconciliation records, explicit ownership metadata, and complete export/handover capability. No single EXVARLİ account, credential, database interface, or service dependency may become the sole means by which a project owner can access or control that project’s funds or records.

TRADIX is responsible for designing, building, testing, and delivering EXVARLİ. After delivery, EXVARLİ remains an independently identifiable product with its own roadmap, data boundaries, and operational records. Its independence also means that its own sale, suspension, or replacement must not transfer ownership of, or interrupt access to, the assets and records of projects it serves.
