# EXVARLİ

**Parent:** TRADIX  
**Project type:** Internal financial operations platform  
**Status:** Project dossier created; implementation not yet started  
**Display name:** EXVARLİ  
**Purpose:** Provide a controlled, auditable financial operations layer for projects developed by TRADIX.

## 1. Mission

EXVARLİ is an internal platform for recording, reconciling, and reporting income generated by projects in the TRADIX portfolio. It is designed to support additional TRADIX projects over time without merging their financial records or identities.

EXVARLİ is not intended to be a public bank, public exchange, payment processor, or custodian for unrelated third parties.

## 2. Initial scope

The initial platform scope is limited to:

- Recording income by source and by project.
- Recording gross amounts, deductions, net amounts, receipts, settlement status, and timestamps.
- Maintaining a traceable audit history for every financial operation.
- Supporting approved provider integrations for receiving funds and, only where lawful and contractually permitted, conversion and transfer to wallets controlled by the relevant project or TRADIX entity.
- Recording conversion provider, exchange rate, fees, asset received, destination address, network, and transaction identifier when applicable.
- Producing project-level records and a consolidated portfolio report without mixing project balances.

## 3. Initial income sources under consideration

- Google advertising revenue generated by NEXORA.
- Amazon affiliate revenue generated by MegaMarket.
- Alibaba affiliate revenue generated by MegaMarket.
- Future revenue sources from other TRADIX projects, added only when explicitly approved.

These are planned integrations, not claims that any provider account or payment route is already connected or approved.

## 4. Accounting and data boundaries

- Each project must have its own financial ledger and source records.
- Portfolio-level reporting may aggregate data but must preserve project attribution.
- Transfers between projects are internal transfers, not new income.
- No project-specific financial account or ledger is to be instantiated until explicitly authorized.
- Every balance must be derivable from recorded, reconciled events; balances must not be silently edited.

## 5. Explicit exclusions for the initial release

- Public customer accounts or wallets.
- Public deposits, withdrawals, or payment services.
- Third-party asset custody.
- Lending, credit, or interest-bearing products.
- A public cryptocurrency exchange.
- Unapproved or unsupported conversion routes.
- Unnecessary features unrelated to the initial internal financial operations mission.

## 6. Compliance boundary

A transparent source of income does not by itself establish that a payment, conversion, custody, or transfer route is lawful or permitted by a provider. Each route must be checked against applicable jurisdictional requirements, provider terms, sanctions restrictions, and asset/network support before activation.

Iranian tax analysis is outside the current project scope, as expressly specified by the project owner.

## 7. Architecture direction

The intended architecture is a shared financial core with separate project ledgers and replaceable integration adapters for income sources, payment providers, and conversion providers. The platform must preserve immutable operation history and produce verifiable reconciliation records.

TRADIX is responsible for designing, building, testing, and delivering EXVARLİ. After delivery, EXVARLİ remains an independently identifiable product with its own roadmap, data boundaries, and operational records.
