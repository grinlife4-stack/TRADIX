# MegaMarket — local project instructions

First read the repository-root `AGENTS.md` and the [Ecosystem Master Map](../../docs/ECOSYSTEM_MASTER_MAP.md). Then read this project's own README, dossier, decision log, and status before making changes.

- **Target:** MegaMarket
- **Classification:** independent product/project
- **Boundary:** MegaMarket owns its product mission, business decisions, data, financial records, accounts, and lifecycle.

MegaMarket identifies and prioritizes its own needs. It may build a capability for its own use, extend an existing shared tool, or contribute a reusable tool/option to the shared toolbox. Other projects may use a contributed capability when it fits their needs; MegaMarket is not required to build for every project, and other projects are not required to adopt what MegaMarket contributes.

Keep MegaMarket's source, data, financial records, accounts, credentials, and decisions separate from all other projects. A shared tool serves through an explicit, scoped interface; it does not own or absorb MegaMarket. Reusable contributions must not expose MegaMarket-specific data, secrets, or private business logic without explicit authorization.

If work crosses into another project or shared tool, identify both sides, the interface, data owner, permissions, maintenance responsibility, and acceptance authority before editing. If unclear, stop and report the ambiguity.
