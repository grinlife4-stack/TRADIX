# Security, privacy and compliance baseline

- Use official APIs and documented scopes. No scraping behind login, CAPTCHA circumvention or terms evasion.
- OAuth tokens and secrets belong in a managed secret store; never in source, browser storage, logs or product records.
- Least privilege, per-provider credentials, rotation and revocation.
- Separate customer, supplier, operator and service identities; enforce tenant isolation server-side.
- Do not collect customer personal data until checkout and fulfillment flows exist and a retention purpose is documented.
- Minimize order data shared with suppliers to what is required to fulfill the specific order.
- Maintain immutable audit events for product eligibility decisions, listing changes, order transitions and financial reconciliation.
- Define data retention, export, deletion, incident response and breach notification for each launch jurisdiction.
- Review consumer protection, tax/VAT/sales tax, import duties, product safety, IP, sanctions, privacy and marketplace rules before launch.
- Never claim legal compliance or marketplace approval without documented review.

## Seller responsibility
Dropshipping does not remove the platform/operator's obligations to buyers. The seller-of-record, delivery, returns, refunds and customer support responsibilities must be explicitly assigned before any live sale.
